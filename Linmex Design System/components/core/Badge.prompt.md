Small uppercase label for categories, statuses and tags; Barlow Condensed caps.

```jsx
<Badge tone="orange" variant="solid">Pre-venta</Badge>
<Badge tone="success" dot>Disponible</Badge>
<Badge tone="navy" variant="soft" shape="square">Departamento</Badge>
```

`variant`: solid | soft | outline. `tone`: orange | navy | neutral | success | warning | danger. `shape`: pill | square. `dot` adds a status dot.
