Real-estate listing card — cover image, status pill, name, location, spec chips and price. Composes `Badge`.

```jsx
<PropertyCard
  image="/assets/imagery/development-street.png"
  status="Disponible" statusTone="success"
  name="Residencial Palmar" location="Colonia Campestre, Mérida"
  specs={["2–3 rec", "120–180 m²", "Entrega 2026"]}
  price="$2.4M MXN" priceNote="desde"
/>
```

Use in grids of developments/units. Lifts and zooms the image on hover.
